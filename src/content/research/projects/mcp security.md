Running research independently means every hour spent chasing an interesting but low-value question is an hour not spent on something that actually matters. The first month of this project was mostly about learning that lesson the hard way.

What ended up working was picking one narrow question per month, writing down what a useful answer would look like before starting, and refusing to move on until that answer existed in some form, even a rough one.

The output after six months is smaller than expected in volume, but every piece of it has been used somewhere, in a blog post, a lab experiment, or a decision about what to build next. That ratio matters more than raw output.
