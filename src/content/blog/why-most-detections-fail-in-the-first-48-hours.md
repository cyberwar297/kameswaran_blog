Most detection programs are not short on alerts, they are short on attention. A rule can fire perfectly and still fail if nobody looks at it in time.

The fix is rarely a new tool. It is usually tighter triage rules, clearer ownership, and dashboards that surface the handful of alerts that matter in the first hour, not the thousand that do not.
